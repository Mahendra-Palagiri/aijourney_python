# Week M — Day 1: The Final Capstone — Charter & Architecture
> Pairs with `1.1.capstone-charter.py` · The graduation project: a complete ML SYSTEM, not a model.

## 🎯 The Bar
Week 8 proved an honest workflow. Week 12 proved deep learning. Week I proved applied modeling. Week M
proves the SYSTEM: problem → data layer → tracked training → registered model → served → monitored →
retrainable → defended. Everything from 20 weeks of addendum, load-bearing, at once.

## 📚 Today
1. **Problem selection** (the hardest part — criteria): real data you can legally use and personally
   understand; a decision worth automating; labels obtainable; temporal arrival structure (so monitoring/
   retraining MEAN something); laptop-feasible. Three candidate problems scored against these; one chosen; the
   runner-up documented as fallback.
2. **The charter** (Week I-1 format, system edition): users, decisions, costs, metric, SLOs (latency,
   freshness, availability — Week K's vocabulary in the requirements now), constraints.
3. **Architecture sketch**: the Week-L lifecycle diagram instantiated for THIS problem — every box mapped to
   a week that taught it; data flow, model family hypotheses (2–3, with Module 2/4 reasoning), serving
   pattern (Week K-4 decision tree, applied), monitoring plan.
4. **Pre-registration** + risk register (Week I discipline, unchanged).

## 🛠️ Deliverables — `weekM_charter.md` (problem, SLOs, architecture diagram, pre-registration, risks)
