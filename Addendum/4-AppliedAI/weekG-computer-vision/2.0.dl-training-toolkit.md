## ⚠️ Common Mistakes
Dropout after the last layer (zeroing logits = chaos) · BN with tiny batch sizes (stats too noisy) · early
stopping on TEST (the eternal sin) · stacking all regularizers blindly on small models (underfitting by enthusiasm).
