# Week I — Day 3: Candidate Models & Disciplined Iteration
> Pairs with `3.1.candidates-iteration.py`

## 📚 Today
1. Field 2–3 serious candidates per your pre-registration (e.g., tuned LGBM / fine-tuned transformer or
   ResNet / domain-specific architecture). No new candidates invented after seeing scores (that's a NEW
   pre-registration, noted as such — amendments are legal, silent ones are not).
2. Iterate on the WORST visible component, not the model du jour: error reading (Weeks E-7/G-4 habit) →
   hypothesis → ONE change → re-measure. Keep an experiment LOG (id, change, hypothesis, result, decision) —
   this log is Module 5-L's experiment-tracking, done by hand one last time.
3. Tune within budget (RandomizedSearch/early stopping as appropriate), preprocessing inside folds, always.
4. Freeze the finalists by protocol (CV mean ± std, stability, cost) BEFORE the holdout.

## 🛠️ Build in the paired .py
Candidate runs through the harness; the experiment log as a committed CSV/markdown; finalist freeze block.

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes
Twenty undocumented experiments (the log or it didn't happen) · iterating on the test set's feedback ·
"just one more candidate" after scores (pre-registration amnesia).
