## 📚 Topics
Consolidation: one real forecasting problem, full honest pipeline, defended with intervals.

## 🎯 Learning Goals
1. Choose real data with stakes you understand (energy use, store sales, web traffic, your own data if you
   have it); define the DECISION the forecast serves (horizon, granularity, asymmetric costs? — the framing
   determines the metric; Week 8 lesson, temporal edition).
2. Pre-register: horizon(s), rolling-origin protocol with gap, metric (MAE? pinball if quantiles matter?),
   baselines, budget.
3. Field: seasonal-naive, best classical (Day 3), LGBM-features (Day 4), one stretch candidate (LSTM or global,
   your call — justify it).
4. Report intervals with verified coverage, not just points (the defense's centerpiece — most forecasts in the
   wild fail exactly here).
5. Write `weekH_defense.md` including the leakage-guard checklist (every trap from Days 1–6 explicitly checked off).
