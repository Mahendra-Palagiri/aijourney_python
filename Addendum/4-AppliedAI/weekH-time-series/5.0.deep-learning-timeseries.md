# Week H — Day 5: Deep Learning for Time Series — LSTM Forecasting (and Honest Doubt)
> Pairs with `5.1.deep-learning-timeseries.py`

## 📚 Topics
Sequence windows as supervised samples, LSTM forecaster (Week E-6's machinery on numbers), normalization
without leakage (again, harder), seq2seq vs single-step heads, the honest comparison vs Day 3–4, when DL
actually wins on time series (and the embarrassing literature on when it doesn't).

## 🎯 Learning Goals
1. Build the windowing Dataset: series → [window_len] input, [horizon] target pairs; the shapes are Week E-5's
   table with features=1 ([B, T, 1]); reuse everything.
2. Scale honestly: fit scaler on TRAIN PERIOD only; inverse-transform predictions for metric comparability
   with Days 3–4 (mixed-scale comparisons are silent nonsense).
3. Train LSTM single-step and direct multi-step heads; apply the FULL Week-G-2 recipe (early stopping on a
   TIME-ordered validation period, not random).
4. Join the league table — LSTM vs LGBM-features vs ARIMA vs seasonal-naive at h=1/6/12 — and accept whatever
   it says (on small univariate series, the classics often hold; that's a FINDING, not a failure).
5. Articulate where DL wins in this domain: many related series (cross-learning), rich exogenous inputs,
   long complex seasonality — i.e., when there's enough signal to feed the parameter count (bias-variance, always).

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes
Scaler fit on the whole series (leakage #4 this week — it never stops) · random-shuffled batches ACROSS the
train/val time boundary · comparing scaled MSE to unscaled MAE (inverse-transform first!).
