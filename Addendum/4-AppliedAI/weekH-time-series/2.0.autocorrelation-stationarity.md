# Week H — Day 2: Autocorrelation & Stationarity — The Statistics of Memory
> Pairs with `2.1.autocorrelation-stationarity.py`

## 📚 Topics
Autocorrelation (ACF) and partial autocorrelation (PACF) — computed and READ, lag plots, stationarity
(why models need it), differencing as the stationarizer, unit-root tests (ADF) at practical level,
white noise as the "nothing left" benchmark.

## 🎯 Learning Goals
1. Define autocorrelation as correlation(series, lagged series) — Week 3's correlation, pointed at the past;
   compute ACF at lag k from scratch before calling statsmodels.
2. Read ACF signatures like residual plots: slow decay = trend; spikes at 12/24/36 = seasonality; all-in-the-
   noise-band = white noise (nothing left to model — the SUCCESS state for residuals!).
3. Get PACF's idea: correlation at lag k AFTER removing what shorter lags explain (regression logic — Week 6's
   "controlling for" instinct); why ACF+PACF together suggest AR/MA orders (the Day-3 bridge).
4. Define stationarity practically (stable mean/variance/autocorrelation over time) and why models that learn
   one regime need it; difference (possibly twice; possibly seasonally) to achieve it; verify with ADF + eyes.
5. Treat white noise as the benchmark: if your model's residuals aren't white noise, structure remains (the
   Ljung-Box idea, used practically).

## 🛠️ Build in the paired .py
1. ACF from scratch (one function); match statsmodels acf; plot with confidence band on three series:
   trending, seasonal, white noise — the signature gallery.
2. PACF plots for the same three; annotate the signatures.
3. Stationarize AirPassengers: log (variance) → diff (trend) → seasonal diff (period); ACF + ADF at each step —
   the staircase to stationarity, documented.
4. Lag scatter plots (t vs t−1, t−12) — correlation made visible.
5. Residual whiteness check function (`acf_is_white(resid)`) — reused every remaining day this week.

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes
Reading ACF of a TRENDING series as "wow everything is correlated" (detrend first; trend dominates) ·
over-differencing (ACF lag-1 strongly negative = you went too far) · treating ADF p-value as sacred (eyes + test, together).
