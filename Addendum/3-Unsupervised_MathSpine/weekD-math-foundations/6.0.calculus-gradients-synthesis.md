## 📚 Topics
Derivative as local linear approximation, partial derivatives & the gradient, the chain rule (PROPERLY, once),
Jacobians at intuition level, gradient descent variants seen mathematically, numerical gradient checking;
synthesis: one map of every math idea in the curriculum.

## 🎯 Learning Goals
1. Define the gradient as the vector of partials AND as "direction of steepest ascent"; show why descent
   steps along −∇L.
2. Re-derive (cleanly, on paper, no peeking): dMSE/dw for linear regression (Week 6) and the (p−y)·x gradient
   for logistic regression (Week 5/qb-week5) — they should take < 10 minutes each now.
3. Write the chain rule for compositions f(g(h(x))) and map it term-by-term onto a 2-layer network's
   backward pass (Week 9 Day 5, revisited with full confidence).
4. Meet the Jacobian as "the matrix of all partials of a vector function" — just enough to read autograd
   docs and Module 4-F papers without flinching.
5. Build numerical gradient checking ((L(w+ε)−L(w−ε))/2ε) — the universal bug detector you'll reuse forever.

parameter shape — your Week-10 instinct) · treating Adam as magic instead of "per-parameter adaptive lr + momentum".
