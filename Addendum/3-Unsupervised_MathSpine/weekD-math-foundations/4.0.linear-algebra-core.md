# Week D — Day 4: Linear Algebra Core — Vectors, Matrices, the Geometric View
> Pairs with `4.1.linear-algebra-core.py` · Prerequisite for Week C Day 4 (PCA) and Module 4-F (attention).

## 📚 Topics
Vectors as arrows AND data rows, dot product (projection + similarity), norms, matrices as
LINEAR TRANSFORMATIONS, matrix multiply as composition, rank, span, linear independence.

## 🎯 Learning Goals
1. Hold both views of a vector simultaneously: geometric arrow ↔ feature row of your DataFrame.
2. Own the dot product three ways: Σaᵢbᵢ (compute), ‖a‖‖b‖cosθ (geometry), "how much of a points along b"
   (projection). Cosine similarity = dot product of unit vectors — write it now; Module 4-E lives on it.
3. See a matrix as a transformation of space: where do the basis vectors land? (columns of the matrix!) —
   the 3Blue1Brown lens, internalized by coding it.
4. Read matrix multiply as composition of transformations and SIMULTANEOUSLY as "every output = dot product
   of a row with a column" — your Week-10 `nn.Linear` is exactly this plus a bias.
5. Define rank/span/independence and connect rank-deficiency to Week-4 multicollinearity and qb-week6 Tricky
   (X'X non-invertible ⇔ perfectly collinear features).

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes
Treating matmul as elementwise (shape discipline from Week 10 saves you) · forgetting matmul order matters
(composition reads right-to-left) · norm confusion (L1 vs L2 — you met them as penalties in Week 5; same objects).
