# Week B — Day 1: Global Feature Importance — and Why Yours Was Probably Biased
> Pairs with `1.1.global-importance.py`

## 📚 Topics
Impurity-based importance and its biases, permutation importance, importance under correlated features,
train-set vs validation-set importance.

## 🎯 Learning Goals
1. Explain how impurity importance is computed (total weighted gain per feature across all splits) and
   its two biases: favors high-cardinality features; computed on TRAIN (reflects memorization too).
2. Explain permutation importance mechanically: shuffle one column on VALIDATION data → score drop = importance.
3. Demonstrate the correlated-features failure: two clones share/steal importance — both methods lie differently.
4. Choose the right tool per question: "what did the model use?" vs "what is predictive?" are different questions.

## 🔍 Core Mechanics
- Impurity importance high-cardinality bias: a random ID-like column gets many candidate split points →
  spurious gain. You will PROVE this with a random noise feature of 100 unique values.
- Permutation importance breaks feature-label link but keeps the feature's marginal distribution; under
  correlation it creates impossible rows (shuffle `fare` independently of `pclass`) → biased toward zero.
- Week-3's logreg coefficients-as-importance: only valid with scaled features, and only linear effects.

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes
